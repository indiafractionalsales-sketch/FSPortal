## 2. Customer Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

### Persona 1: Founder Frank / CEO Chitra ("On Behalf Of" - OBO)
- **Role & Profile:** Founder/CEO at a Series A/B B2B SaaS startup or high-growth industrial manufacturing SME (\$1M–\$15M ARR).
- **Core Jobs-to-be-Done:**
  - "Help me test and scale sales in a new industry vertical or geography without taking on high fixed compensation risk."
  - "Give me instant access to verified sales leaders with existing enterprise networks."
- **Key Pain Points:**
  - High mis-hire risk (\$180K average loss per failed VP Sales hire).
  - Slow recruitment cycles (60–90 days to hire + 90 days ramp time).
  - Lack of visibility into remote/contractor daily activity and lead pipelines.

### Persona 2: Sales Director Sam (Fractional Sales Partner - SP)
- **Role & Profile:** Senior B2B Sales Leader, former VP of Sales, or Enterprise BD Director with 10+ years of domain experience.
- **Core Jobs-to-be-Done:**
  - "Help me build a \$200K+ portfolio career serving 3–4 non-competing clients simultaneously."
  - "Protect my commission payouts with legally binding agreements and automated deal ledgers."
- **Key Pain Points:**
  - Administrative overhead in sourcing high-intent client offers.
  - Delayed or defaulted commission payments by clients.
  - Absence of dedicated fractional sales management software.

### Persona 3: Field BD Representative (On-Ground SDR)
- **Role & Profile:** Field Sales Specialist executing on-ground client visits and lead generation.
- **Core Jobs-to-be-Done:**
  - "Verify my field check-ins and log new client leads effortlessly."
  - "Ensure my daily allowances and performance bonuses are processed transparently."
- **Key Pain Points:** Disputed location check-ins, tedious manual CRM entry, delayed reimbursements.
